Admission Contract

The boundary between validated distillation output and durable canonical project memory.

Current status

This record reflects project-chat-handoff commit 5ee7663e. The admission contract and its ten-case pressure test are accepted as the current design direction for the experimental distiller work. They do not alter shipped engine behavior or the frozen PEMS/COVE authority by themselves.

Boundary

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

admission evaluation
      ↓

      ↓
canonical memory lifecycle

Structural validity is necessary but not sufficient for admission. Admission records lifecycle standing under project policy; it does not manufacture truth, empirical grounding, derivation, uncertainty, or normative authority.

Core rules

  • Admission outcomes are lifecycle metadata, not proposition semantics.
  • Resolved provenance and source authority must remain intact; admission cannot invent normative standing.
  • Derived records retain their premise graph. Connected candidates may require transactional admission.
  • Conflicts remain explicit rather than silently overwriting canonical history.
  • Equivalent candidates reconcile to existing canonical identity rather than duplicating memory.
  • Supersession is append-preserving; recency alone does not establish supersession.
  • Uncertainty and assumptions may be durable memory without being promoted into facts.

Evaluation result

The initial contract was pressure-tested across ten lifecycle cases and preserved the intended distinctions without requiring a change to the distillation ontology.

The evaluation specifically confirmed that unresolved provenance blocks grounded admission, conflicts are preserved, derived records with noncanonical premises require transactional handling or remain provisional, and normative standing cannot be created by the admission mechanism.

What remains unresolved

Automatic admission policy is intentionally deferred. The next work is PEMS mapping/reconciliation design and shadow operation to determine which candidate classes, if any, can be admitted deterministically without semantic reinterpretation or authority leakage.

Authority boundary: this experimental admission layer may govern how validated candidates enter project memory, but it does not itself change canonical PEMS/COVE contracts, admit new project decisions, or alter current main.
